# SAM Research Project Stewardship Declaration

**Document version:** v1.0  
**Date:** 2026-07-10  
**Originator:** Sean Brady  
**Entity:** SAM Research Project LC

## Purpose

This declaration records the stewardship intent carried by the Substrate
Accumulation Model (SAM), the Substrate Ledger Computer (SLC), and work,
apparatus, methods, inventions, or derivatives enabled by them.

If SAM-derived work produces commercial value, that value carries a public
responsibility with it. The work is intended to create benefit beyond the
holding entity and its immediate commercial participants.

## Founder Intent

The originator believes SAM may be correct, while recognizing that the
research may also be wrong. This declaration is not a claim that SAM has been
proved. It records the intended responsibility that would follow if the model,
the Substrate Ledger Computer (SLC), or related work produces meaningful
technological value.

The SLC is viewed as having the potential for a significant technological
breakthrough. If that potential becomes real, the intended direction of value
is outward rather than upward: toward people and communities, rather than
toward a private payoff for the originator or a small group of holders.

The ninety-percent figure in this declaration is a stewardship floor, not the
founder's personal target. The founder's stated intention is to take no payoff
from commercialization and to direct all distributable value outward through
the public-benefit structure, after the documented costs, taxes, and continued
research and development described below have been met.

This intention is deliberately meant to leave the founder's personal control.
The Courtroom provenance record, technical hashes, patent records, licensing
terms, entity governance, and successor obligations are intended to carry the
stewardship forward. Hashes preserve provenance and priority; the legal
instruments and governance terms must carry the operative obligation.

## Stewardship Principle

For commercial value received by SAM Research Project LC or a successor entity
in consideration for use, licensing, sale, acquisition, equity transfer, or
other commercialization of SAM-derived work, no less than ninety percent (90%)
of net proceeds is intended to flow to public-benefit purposes.

For this declaration, net proceeds means value received after reasonable and
documented operating costs, applicable taxes, and good-faith reinvestment in
continued research and development. The definition is not intended to permit
accounting arrangements designed to dilute the stewardship flow.

## Public-Benefit Intent

The intended vehicle is the SAM Foundation, or an equivalent public-benefit
structure if a foundation cannot be established or maintained. Priority areas
include housing, recovery, food security, clean water, medical access,
education, opportunity, and other community charity consistent with the
founder's intent.

SAM-derived value is intended to flow outward toward people and communities
that need help most, including communities outside the country of incorporation.

## Continuity

This stewardship intent is meant to survive licensing, sale, merger,
acquisition, equity events, asset transfers, change of control, dissolution,
bankruptcy, and succession. A party receiving or commercializing SAM-derived
rights is expected to accept the stewardship obligation as part of receiving
those rights.
