# SAM public-benefit stewardship

Sean Brady's stewardship direction applies to SAM, the Substrate Ledger
Computer, and the commercial value received by SAM Research Project LC or
its successor from SAM-derived work.

The [original declaration](legal/STEWARDSHIP_DECLARATION_ORIGINAL.md), dated
2026-07-10, is preserved without alteration. Its digest is recorded in
provenance/SOURCE_MANIFEST.json. The original declaration is provenance; links
inside that historical copy refer to the originating research workspace.

## The commitment

No less than **90% of net proceeds received by SAM Research Project LC or a
successor entity** from the specified commercialization is intended to flow to
the SAM Foundation or an equivalent public-benefit structure. Net proceeds
means receipts after reasonable, documented operating costs, applicable taxes
and good-faith reinvestment in continuing research and development. Accounting
arrangements designed to dilute the public-benefit allocation are contrary to
the declaration.

The founder's stated intention is to take no personal commercialization payoff
and direct all distributable value outward after the stated costs and ongoing
research needs. The 90% figure is the declared floor.

Priority purposes include housing, recovery, food security, clean water,
medical access, education and opportunity, including communities outside the
country of incorporation.

## Putting the commitment into effect

The intended continuity includes licensing, sale, acquisition, change of
control and succession. Signed commercial agreements and the relevant entity
governance instruments must carry the allocation, accounting, oversight,
beneficiary and successor obligations. A hash preserves the declaration's
identity; enforceable legal instruments implement the obligation.

This repository does not state that a foundation has been incorporated, that
rights have been assigned to an entity, or that every contemplated successor
obligation has already been executed. The rights register records documents
actually supplied for this release.

## Relationship to the open spin licenses

The scoped spin release is available under MIT for software and CC BY 4.0 for research materials, as defined in [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md). Commercial use does not require a separate permission or donation. The 90% commitment concerns the specified receipts of SAM Research Project LC or its successor; it adds no condition, royalty or revenue allocation to downstream MIT/CC BY users. The original declaration remains preserved as owner-governance provenance.
