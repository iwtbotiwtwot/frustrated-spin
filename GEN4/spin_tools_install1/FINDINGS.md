# What the shared spin installation adds to GEN4

The completed spin work is now useful in three ways: as exact source knowledge,
as reusable execution and planning methods, and as a shared finite-factor tool
for explicitly mapped questions in other domains. GEN4 can retrieve completed
spectra, compute a changed or new declared source, retain its conditional
responses, and recover the result through its authenticated session machinery.

**The test result suggests strong contact with the concept.** The qualification
exercises these capabilities through the installed operations, including fresh
arithmetic and actual research-pod CUDA execution. The conclusions below concern
those demonstrated capabilities and their mathematical uses.

## 1. A complete source collection, with meaningful identities

The default roster contains all sizes N1–N96, N100, N105 and N120: **99 default
entries**. An additional named historical N120 ring brings the source-family
registry to **100 records**. All 97 preceding catalog references remain intact.
N100 and N105 retain their original packet/component sources; the current N120
retains its connected five-regular extension of N96. Their graph identities and
source meanings remain explicit. [Source inventory](SOURCE_SUMMARY.md)

The two N120 records make source identity especially concrete. Each has 120
vertices and 300 couplings, but the current frontier has ground energy −434
with degeneracy 8, while the historical ring has ground energy −430 with
degeneracy 2. Their occupied energy-bin counts are 435 and 421, respectively.
Consequently, N and edge count are useful descriptors, while the full graph
and its source hash identify the actual calculation. The registry now makes
both sources selectable without overwriting either.

The retained material includes exact spectra, boundary operators, actual
transitions, alternative plans, learned policies and their source bindings.
These objects answer different questions. A spectrum supplies the completed
distribution; a plan tells the solver how to recompute it; a transition records
what changed; a model ranks candidate work. Keeping these roles explicit makes
the collection useful for new research rather than only historical lookup.

## 3. Conditional operators preserve the information needed for reuse

A scalar density of states counts configurations at each energy after summing
over boundary states. The conditional rows retain that boundary information.
For a retained boundary configuration (b), write its exact counts as

\[
D_b(E)=\#\{s:E(s)=E,\ s_{\partial}=b\}.
\]

The scalar DOS is (D(E)=\sum_bD_b(E)). Once those rows have been summed, the
scalar result generally cannot reconstruct their individual responses.
The installed operations therefore preserve ordered port identities, expose
their availability, and permit exact conditioning and rational readout.

The N96→N120 transition is an existing concrete example: 24 old boundary
vertices are touched, whereas the saved N96 operator retains four ports.
That transition reuses structural knowledge while computing the new spectrum
from the complete new source. N100 and N105, in contrast, retain all 16 rows
for their declared four-role boundary. The historical N120 ring currently
supplies scalar DOS; its archived transfer-state labels are not interchangeable
with those full-source conditional rows. [Capability map](CAPABILITY_MAP.md),
[N120 source result](../frustrated_spin_n120_frontier1/RESULT.md)

An immediate mathematical use of the retained rows is a boundary-supported
energy change. If a proposed modification has an explicitly derived energy
increment \(\delta E(b)\) depending only on the retained boundary, then its
new scalar distribution is

\[
D_{\mathrm{new}}(E')=\sum_b D_b\!\left(E'-\delta E(b)\right).
\]

This identity identifies a constructive next reuse operation: shift and combine
the already exact boundary rows, then compare with a fresh solve of the modified
graph. It is an analytic implication of the stored operator; this installation
does not report a new execution of that proposed update. Changes involving
unretained interior information call for a richer retained boundary or a fresh
calculation.

## 4. Signed finite factors provide the direct shared computational connection

`GEN3_SPIN_CONTRACT` evaluates an explicit finite sum of products. Variables
have declared finite domains, factors have exact signed integer tables, and
chosen variables remain open in the output. Nonbinary states, isolated variables
and exact cancellation are supported. This same mathematical operation can
serve distinct domain questions when their source data supply the factors.

The qualification used the same declared synthetic factors through
MATTER_SEARCH, ATOM3D, STARBREAKER and RH. Every route returned the exact signed
table `[9, -14, 18]`, with total 13. Separate checks exercised full cancellation,
an isolated ternary variable and independent enumeration of a nonbinary factor
network. These demonstrate shared execution and sign preservation; they do not
assign a domain meaning to the synthetic data.
[Integration qualification](qualification-gen3-integration/QUALIFICATION.json),
[exact method](EXACT_METHOD.md)

That distinction makes the cross-domain use practical:

- **Volume II:** an explicitly supplied classical or diagonal interaction
  model can use exact DOS, ground sectors and conditional responses. The
  existing noncommuting exchange tools remain the appropriate operations when
  the source requires those operators.
- **Volume I:** finite response tables can be conditioned and reweighted
  exactly. A declared finite history model can also be written as a factor
  contraction over intermediate states. Physical timing requires the actual
  transition or propagation law supplied by the question.
- **RH:** finite arithmetic factors can be contracted with their signs intact.
  The useful next connection is an existing source-bound finite expression
  whose decomposition admits reusable intermediate factors. The present
  installation contributes that computation facility; it adds no new uniform
  cancellation result.
- **General computation:** exact state elimination, polynomial convolution,
  boundary retention and authenticated result reuse are now available through
  the shared interface rather than tied to one historical spin campaign.

There is also a useful structural connection to the earlier CMB information-loss
work. Summing spin boundary labels and summing directional covariance information
both produce coarser readouts. Their variables and physical meanings differ,
but both examples show why the retained object must match the later question.
The constructive lesson is to retain the response channels needed for a planned
comparison. [CMB analysis](../full_web_expansion1/cmb/RESULT.md)

## 5. Learned plans, exact answers and reusable state now work together

The installed solver can call the source-aware selector, reuse an
identical-source mathematical plan, or use an explicitly selected focused native
policy. The integration check exercised the focused pairwise model on a changed
source and recorded its predictions. When a component-family predecessor could
not supply a CUDA ordering, the selector explicitly identified N96 as the
planning anchor. This preserves the distinction between a useful computational
anchor and the actual scientific source. [Selection receipt](qualification-gen3-integration/FOCUSED_SELECTION.json)

The earlier cost models retain their 14-MIG calibration. Their learned ordering
can be examined under an explicit transfer scope, while new-device timing is
measured as new evidence. The exact source and coefficients remain fixed across
candidate plans.

Recovery is equally explicit. Published exact results survive a reopened native
session. The GPU checkpoint qualification recovered all 80 completed modular
tasks, executed zero new arithmetic tasks, and reconstructed the same answer.
That receipt identifies both inherited and fresh task counts, making reuse
distinguishable from recomputation. [GPU recovery](pod_final/qualification-gen4-integration/GPU_RECOVERY.json)

## 6. Evidence and recommended next uses

The workstation passed **109 source checks, 16 exact-tool checks and 14
integration checks**. The research pod passed **109, 16 and 18**, respectively;
its additional checks exercised actual CUDA computation and checkpoint
recovery. The source checks retrieved completed material without recomputing
historical spectra. Fresh N100/N105, changed-source computation and independent
small references were separately executed.
[Local source checks](qualification-gen3-sources/RESULT.json),

[pod exact checks](pod_final/qualification-gen4-exact/QUALIFICATION.json),

Three next uses follow directly from this installation:

1. **Use the shared solver for a new declared source.** Preserve its family,
   couplings, fields and ports, inspect the selected structural method, and
   retain the resulting spectrum as a new exact object.
2. **Develop boundary-supported updates from an existing conditional operator.**
   The shift identity above gives a precise first case with a fresh-solve
   comparison and a clear condition for when the stored boundary is sufficient.
3. **Map one existing finite expression from another active domain into signed
   factors.** Compare its exact output with that domain's current route, then
   determine which intermediate factors can be retained for subsequent work.

These uses build on demonstrated methods and retained sources. They turn the
completed spin campaigns into tools for further questions while keeping each
new scientific mapping visible and reviewable.
