# Fully connected completions of the N300 packet sources

Sean Brady is originator and conceptual director. OpenAI ChatGPT and Codex are
AI research collaborators. This additive source-construction project responds
to the request to generate a fully connected graph from the existing work.

For each of the three retained N300 source families, generate two explicit
complete graphs: preserve every original interaction and fill every absent
pair with either +1 or -1. Both choices are agent-selected comparison
constructions; no coupling choice is attributed to an owner scientific premise.
All fields and the ordered retained ports are preserved. Each graph has
44,850 nonzero interactions. Canonical and packet source records remain unchanged.

The source generator runs through a MATTER_SEARCH DomainSession. It verifies
pair coverage, preservation of every original interaction, and the energy
identity below on sixteen deterministic assignments per generated graph.
These are graph-generation and identity-check records. No full N300 density
of states has been calculated for these dense completions in this project.

## Exact connection to the existing packet method

Let J0 be the uniform coupling assigned to absent pairs, M=sum(s_i), and E0
the parent edge set. The complete-graph energy can be written exactly as

    E = -J0*(M^2-N)/2 - sum_(u,v in E0)((J_uv-J0)*s_u*s_v) - sum_i(h_i*s_i).

This follows directly from M^2=N+2*sum_(u<v)(s_u*s_v).
The representation accounts for every added interaction. For disconnected
packet parents, its correction interactions remain confined to the packets.
For the connected-chain parent, correction bridge interactions may remain.

An exact computational route is therefore to retain each packet's joint
count table over correction energy, packet magnetization, and ordered ports;
combine the tables; then map each total magnetization to its collective energy
shift. Remaining chain corrections additionally require gateway-conditioned
messages. The existing energy-only tables do not contain all of this joint
information, so these local tables must be constructed explicitly.

This route is derived here as a next implementation, not installed or timed.
Uniform completion of the positive packet graph with +1 gives the familiar
uniform complete-graph interaction, whose energy depends only on M. Signed
parents and negative completion retain different correction structure and
provide additional dense frustrated test cases. Arbitrary edge-by-edge new
couplings would require their own representation and cost analysis.

The running consecutive packet campaign on the pod is unchanged.
